# AtomDrops Repair Services Module — Complete Master Prompt

## Core Concept

The Repair Module connects:

- Customers who need something repaired
- Repair Technicians who provide services
- Admins who supervise quality, disputes, and platform operations

The platform should support:

- Electronics repair
- Home appliance repair
- Furniture repair
- Mobile repair
- Laptop/computer repair
- Air conditioner repair
- TV repair
- Washing machine repair
- Refrigerator repair
- Plumbing
- Electrical work
- Home maintenance services
- Other service categories

The entire experience should feel professional, trustworthy, transparent, and easy to use.

---

# Customer Journey

## 1. Customer Opens Repair Marketplace

Customer enters the Repair Services section.

They can:

- Browse service categories
- Search services
- Search technicians
- View popular services
- View nearby technicians
- View top-rated technicians
- View recently completed jobs
- View estimated service costs

## 2. Choose Service Type

Customer selects a service category and views common repair types, estimated pricing ranges, typical repair duration, and available technicians.

## 3. Create Repair Request

Fields include:

### Problem Information

- Service category
- Device/item type
- Brand
- Model
- Problem title
- Detailed description

### Media Upload

- Photos
- Videos
- Documents
- Warranty papers

### Service Location

- Home visit
- Workshop drop-off
- Pickup service

### Address Information

- Full address
- Landmark
- Map location
- Contact number

### Preferred Schedule

- Preferred date
- Preferred time slot
- Flexible schedule option

## 4. Request Submission

The system publishes the repair request and matching technicians receive notifications.

---

# Technician Journey

## 5. Technician Receives Request

Technician can:

- Accept
- Reject
- Ignore

## 6. Technician Sends Proposal

Proposal contains:

- Estimated price
- Estimated completion time
- Required parts
- Service notes
- Visit charge
